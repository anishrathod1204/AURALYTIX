# AURALYTIX product and launch requirements

## Product presentation

- Keep the interface specific, calm, and technically credible.
- Use the AURALYTIX charcoal, warm neutral, and restrained amber palette.
- Do not use purple gradients, pill-shaped buttons, emoji icons, fake reviews, fake customer counters, or fabricated metrics.
- Do not use vague hero copy, AI-generated stock imagery, cursor animation, or decorative scroll animation.
- Keep motion limited to interaction feedback and respect reduced-motion settings.
- Charts, maps, source counts, and other data displays must be based on returned provider data.
- Do not claim that an integration is connected unless the backend reports it as configured.

## Required before public launch

- Connect and verify the custom domain.
- Keep the AURALYTIX favicon in place.
- Remove any platform badge such as “Made with AI” if one is added by the hosting platform.
- Publish a reviewed privacy policy and terms and conditions.
- Complete the operator identity, support contact, governing law, actual provider list, hosting and log retention details, and data retention details in the legal pages.
- Verify all API credentials are stored as deployment secrets and are not present in source, examples, or archives.

Do not publish or deploy the site while any launch requirement above is incomplete.
